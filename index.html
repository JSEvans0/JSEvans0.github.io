How I work through a dataset

  1. Ask the question first. What decision is this analysis supposed to support?
  2. Inspect and clean. Nulls and outliers handled case by case, based on what each missing value actually means.
  3. Explore. Distributions and correlations, before assuming anything.
  4. Model or measure. Always against a baseline, so “good” means something.
  5. Say what to do about it. A finding without a recommendation is unfinished.
